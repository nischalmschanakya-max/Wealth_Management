📌 Regular SIP (No Step-Up)
—

Gain: —
Same SIP every month forever. Simple — but your income grows while your investment stays flat.
🚀 Step-Up SIP (Annual Increase)
—
Invested: —
Extra vs Regular: —
SIP increases yearly. Each new rupee added early compounds for longer.
📐 Why It Works
Each year's increased SIP compounds on a larger base. The effect grows exponentially, not linearly. Mathematics works in your favour.
✅ The Rule
Link step-up to your salary hike. Get a 12% raise? Step up SIP by 10%. You barely notice the lifestyle impact but the compounding impact over 20 years is massive.
⚠️ Common Mistake
Most people spend every rupee of each raise. Committing just 50% of each raise to SIP step-up creates vastly more wealth long-term.
